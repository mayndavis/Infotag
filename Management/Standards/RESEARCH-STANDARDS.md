# Research and Evidence Standards

1. **Traceability:** cite the original source for every material claim.
2. **Source hierarchy:** prefer regulatory filings, official company releases, earnings materials, and primary datasets; use reputable independent reporting for context.
3. **Date discipline:** record publication date, event date where different, and the date a financial or market figure was measured.
4. **Evidence labels:** distinguish verified fact, company guidance, analyst estimate, forecast, rumour, and interpretation.
5. **Numerical integrity:** include currency, units, period, comparison basis, and methodology. Do not mix market snapshots from different dates without saying so.
6. **Balanced analysis:** explain positive implications, negative implications, risks, and uncertainties.
7. **No false precision:** label approximations and scenarios. Do not present forecasts as outcomes.
8. **Corrections:** preserve the original publication and append a dated correction or superseding version.
9. **Copyright:** summarize and link to third-party material; do not upload copyrighted reports unless redistribution is permitted.
10. **Investment caution:** research is informational, not a personalized buy/sell recommendation.
