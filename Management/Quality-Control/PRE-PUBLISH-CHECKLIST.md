# Pre-publication Checklist

- [ ] Coverage window and publication date are explicit.
- [ ] Provider identity is recorded in metadata.
- [ ] Material claims have source links and dates.
- [ ] Facts, estimates, forecasts, rumours, and interpretations are clearly distinguished.
- [ ] Figures include units, currencies, periods, and measurement dates.
- [ ] Positive and negative implications are both considered.
- [ ] No empty placeholder topic folders were created.
- [ ] New relevant companies or sectors were not excluded merely because they are absent from an older list.
- [ ] No secrets, personal data, or unauthorized copyrighted files are included.
- [ ] Corrections and uncertainties are disclosed.
- [ ] GitHub commit and published paths have been verified.
