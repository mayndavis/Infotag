## Data taxonomy

Use these categories when applicable, but instantiate only those needed by the actual findings:

- `02-Master-Reports/`: Technology-Market, AI-and-Technology, Market-Research.
- `03-Company-Research/`: company-specific findings. Create a company directory only when material coverage exists; new companies are allowed.
- `04-Industry-Analysis/`: AI, semiconductors, cloud, data centres, networking, energy and cooling, emerging technologies, or other relevant sectors.
- `05-Investment-Outlook/`: multi-year outlooks, company outlooks, market trends and risk analysis.
- `06-Sources-and-References/`: source register and source material actually used.
- `07-Archive/`: corrections, superseded items and historical material when needed.

The numbering preserves the conceptual taxonomy. It does not require every category to appear in every dated update.
