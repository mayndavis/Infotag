# NVIDIA Update: 24 November 2026

**Coverage window:** 2026-11-23 08:33:54 EAT to 2026-11-24 08:42:29 EAT  
**Primary sources:** SEC Form 8-K and Exhibit 99.1  
**Independent reporting:** Reuters  
**Evidence labels:** Confirmed filing; company statement; independent reporting; analyst interpretation.

## 1. CEO reinstatement

NVIDIA's Form 8-K, filed on 23 November, states that the board reinstated Jen-Hsun (Jensen) Huang as President, CEO and board member, effective immediately. The filing identifies 22 November 2026 as the date of the board action. Colette Kress continues as Executive Vice President and CFO. [S1]

The accompanying 23 November press release says the board voted unanimously after discussions with Huang. NVIDIA says the board received input from senior U.S. government officials and concluded that the reinstatement poses no national-security risk. This is the company's stated conclusion; it is not a public finding that every related investigation has ended. [S2]

The same release says:
- the independent-director investigation into reports of a foreign-intelligence effort targeting Huang will continue, with independent counsel retained;
- a National Security and Export Control Committee has been formed, composed solely of independent directors;
- NVIDIA is beginning a search for a Chief Compliance Officer who will report to the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee; and
- the company will continue cooperating with government investigations and inquiries. [S2]

Reuters reported that the board reversed its earlier position less than a week after the chair had said Huang would not be rehired. Governance experts questioned the board's independence while its investigation remained unfinished. [S3]

## 2. Market reaction

Reuters reported NVIDIA shares rose 31.5% on 23 November, restoring nearly $1 trillion in market value. The same report said the gain was the largest daily percentage rise in 23 years. This is market-price movement, not a change in the company's cash balance or operating guidance. [S4, S5]

The 24 November Asian-session reaction was broad: Taiwan's Taiex rose 9.1%, TSMC and Foxconn approached daily price limits, the KOSPI rose 6.5%, and Japan's Nikkei gained as much as 5%. These were early-session observations in the Reuters report, not final market closes. [S5]

## 3. Export-control exposure

BIS filed a public-inspection final rule on 23 November adding seven entities in Singapore, Malaysia and Thailand to the Entity List. The rule cites significant risk of diversion of advanced-computing commodities and requires licences for all items subject to the Export Administration Regulations, with a presumption of denial. The named entities include Megaspeed International Pte. Ltd. and six other entities in the region. The rule is a regulatory designation based on risk, not a criminal conviction. [S6]

The U.S. Departments of Commerce and Justice said Commerce began investigating Megaspeed in 2024, requested NVIDIA sales records in May 2025, and that NVIDIA put Megaspeed on its internal no-sell list in June 2025. U.S. and Singaporean officials conducted an on-site end-use check in June 2025, after which DOJ took the lead role in the criminal investigation. [S7]

Reuters reported that Singapore's investigation of Megaspeed remains ongoing. Singapore's prime minister said a previous inquiry into Alice Huang found no basis for prosecution; that is not a conclusion about the separate U.S. investigation of Megaspeed. [S9]

## 4. Investor interpretation

**Positive:** The return of NVIDIA's co-founder reduces immediate uncertainty around executive leadership and product-roadmap continuity.

**Negative:** The ongoing board investigation, new compliance structures, government scrutiny and regional Entity List action keep governance and export-control risks elevated. The board's assurance about national-security risk should not be treated as a substitute for the completion of investigations.

**Cash flow and ROIC:** The available sources do not quantify lost revenue, receivables, inventory exposure, compliance spending or capex changes from these events. Do not infer a financial impact from the share-price rebound alone. Monitor channel controls, customer concentration, export compliance, product execution and free-cash-flow conversion.

**1–5 year implication:** NVIDIA's execution advantage remains important, but durable investor confidence will also depend on credible governance, auditable customer access and continued monetisation of deployed AI infrastructure.
