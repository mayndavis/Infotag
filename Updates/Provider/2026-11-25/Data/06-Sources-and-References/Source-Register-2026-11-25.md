## Source-quality notes

- S1 and S2 are primary Dell disclosures. They largely reproduce the same results release; they are two documents, not two independent confirmations of every statement.
- Dell's fiscal Q3 ended on 2026-10-30. The release date is 2026-11-24. The report keeps the fiscal period and publication date distinct.
- Dell's $132.3 billion AI backlog and $60.2 billion of orders are company-reported. Backlog is not treated as recognised revenue or guaranteed cash flow.
- Dell's free cash flow is a company non-GAAP measure. Adjusted free cash flow is a separate measure with additional adjustments for financing receivables and equipment under operating leases.
- Reuters S3 reports that Dell AI servers are powered by NVIDIA chips. It does not quantify NVIDIA's share of Dell purchases or the value of any particular NVIDIA order.
- S4 is Reuters' account of a Goldman Sachs research note. The note itself was not retrieved, so the 52 GW figure remains an attributed analyst estimate, not a verified outcome.
- S5 reports CDS market observations and a model-derived default-probability estimate. These are market-risk indicators, not actual defaults or certain predictions.
- S6 reports a third-party Enverus study. The original study was not directly accessed; its spending-normalised comparison is not treated as a complete measurement of absolute or local water impact.
- No aggregate claim count or 100% verification label is asserted.
