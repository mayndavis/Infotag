# NVIDIA Update: Governance, Credit Rating and Export-Control Scrutiny

**Publication date:** 2026-11-26  
**Coverage window:** 2026-11-25 08:49:02 EAT to 2026-11-26 08:27:03 EAT

## 1. S&P places AA rating on CreditWatch Negative

S&P Global Ratings' official ratings-actions listing showed NVIDIA's long-term rating at **AA with CreditWatch Negative** following a 25 November 2026 action. The rating was not downgraded during this window. [S1]

Reuters reported that S&P's concerns include board independence, executive oversight, succession planning and whether governance practices have kept pace with NVIDIA's growth. Reuters said S&P could lower the rating by one notch within 90 days if it concludes the concerns remain unresolved. [S2]

Moody's retained its **Aa1 rating and stable outlook**. It said Jensen Huang's return reduced near-term uncertainty about leadership and strategic direction, while governance and regulatory uncertainty remained. [S3]

**Interpretation:** this is a governance and oversight warning, not evidence of deteriorating near-term debt service capacity. A CreditWatch action is not a completed downgrade. S&P and Moody's differ in emphasis: S&P is testing board and management oversight, while Moody's highlighted the reduction in leadership uncertainty.

## 2. US intelligence reporting remains unresolved

Reuters reported that people briefed on US intelligence assessments described differing views among officials about Alice Huang's contacts with Chinese intelligence. The FBI assessment, as described by those sources, did not show that she was an officer or asset of Chinese intelligence. The CIA assessment was described as not clearly showing that she possessed sensitive national-security information or passed it to China, while leaving open the possibility of new evidence. Reuters said it could not determine the details of the intelligence reports. These are attributed accounts of classified assessments, not public agency findings. They do not establish espionage or wrongdoing. [S5]

TIME separately reported that three Chinese state entities had indirect historical investment totalling 1.9% in Megaspeed's former parent, 7Road Holdings. TIME said the remaining ownership picture was unclear and described a complex chain of corporate transfers and rebranding. Experts quoted by TIME said the history warranted enhanced due diligence; the report does not establish current state ownership, current control or unlawful conduct. [S6]

## 3. Why this matters to investors

**Risks**
- Governance uncertainty can affect board credibility, succession planning and the market's assessment of key-person dependence.
- Export-control and customer-due-diligence concerns can increase compliance costs and constrain access to some customers or regions.
- Reputational issues can influence government procurement, counterparties and financing terms even without a formal legal finding.

**Counterweights**
- NVIDIA remained rated AA by S&P during this window; no downgrade occurred.
- Moody's retained Aa1 with a stable outlook.
- The reporting does not establish that NVIDIA knowingly violated export controls or that sensitive information was transferred to China.

## 4. 1–5 year watchpoints

1. S&P's decision within its review period and any public explanation of remedial actions.
2. Changes to board composition, independent oversight, succession planning and compliance leadership.
3. Findings from the company's independent export-control and national-security review.
4. Changes in licensing, customer-screening and regional sales exposure.
5. Evidence that governance issues are affecting customer demand, government relationships, financing or commercial execution.

A possible one-notch downgrade to AA- would still be high-grade investment territory. The more consequential risk is whether governance and compliance issues eventually affect reputation, customer access or future growth.

No new NVIDIA operating-results release was verified in this coverage window. This report concerns credit, governance and regulatory risk, not a change to the company's previously reported operating figures.
