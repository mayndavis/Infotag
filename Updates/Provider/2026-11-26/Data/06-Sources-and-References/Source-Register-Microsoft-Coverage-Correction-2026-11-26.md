## Verification notes

- Reuters published S1 at 16:11 UTC on 24 November 2026. This is 19:11 EAT, ten minutes after the previous scan's stated coverage window began at 19:01 EAT. The article therefore fell inside the 25 November scan window.
- S1 is an independent report based on internal memos reviewed by Reuters. The internal memos are not public primary sources in this register; details from them remain attributed to Reuters.
- Hansard S2 is a primary parliamentary record. It confirms the incident and the UK government's stated impact assessment, but it does not independently verify every internal engineering detail reported by Reuters.
- The minister said critical services such as 999, accident and emergency departments, borders and transport remained operational. The record also includes questions from MPs about reported NHS trust disruption and local service impacts; do not overstate the absence of all public-service impact.
- The sources do not quantify Microsoft revenue loss, churn, SLA credits, remediation cost or Azure capex changes.
- This supplement corrects an omission in the previous coverage. It does not present the outage as a new development in the 26 November scan window.
