# Multi-Year Technology Investment Scenarios (2027–2031): 2026-11-21

**Prepared:** 2026-11-21 | **Coverage Window:** 2026-11-18 to 2026-11-21  

## 1. Scenario Framework

Infotag evaluates technology investments across three discrete structural scenarios:

### Scenario A — High-Productivity Acceleration (Bull Case)
Enterprise agentic workflows deliver demonstrable margin expansion across non-tech sectors, sustaining hyperscaler capex growth at >25% CAGR through 2028. Silicon packaging bottlenecks resolve as TSMC AP8 and Intel 18A foundry nodes reach commercial volume.

### Scenario B — Normalizing Capital Discipline (Base Case)
AI infrastructure investment transitions from speculative pre-building toward utilization-gated deployments. Disaggregated inference architectures lower token TCO by 35%, prioritizing power efficiency and optical interconnects. Leading software platforms capture steady monetization, while pure-play compute hardware multiples normalize.

### Scenario C — Capex Digestion & Margin Compression (Pessimistic Case)
Hyperscaler depreciation headwinds outpace enterprise software revenue conversion, triggering a multi-quarter pause in server hardware purchases. Merchant accelerator pricing power contracts as custom internal silicon (TPU, Trainium) matures.

## 2. Institutional Portfolio Implications

Investors should emphasize companies with resilient multi-pillar balance sheets, proprietary advanced packaging agreements, and verifiable cost-per-token advantages.
