## Why an interim publication is warranted

Reuters published an investigation on 27 November reporting a breach of Cloud One, the U.S. Air Force's flagship cloud platform. The report describes operational disruption and an alleged control-plane access path, but the attacker, vulnerability, affected provider layer, scope and any data theft remain unknown. The Air Force did not confirm or deny the specific incident. CBS News later obtained additional Air Force and Department of Defense comments.

This development is material to government-cloud security, multi-cloud operations and the trust requirements of mission-critical cloud procurement. It does not establish that Microsoft Azure, AWS, Google Cloud or Oracle Cloud was itself breached.

The normal 72-hour publication interval from the 25 November routine update was due at 2026-11-28 08:49:02 EAT. The current scan cutoff was 08:16:45 EAT, so this is an interim material-event publication rather than the routine update.

## Priority-company coverage

Microsoft, Amazon and Alphabet are relevant because their cloud services are named in Reuters' description of Cloud One. Oracle is also named. The reporting does not identify which provider or layer was affected. No new company-specific operating disclosure or filing was verified for the priority companies during this window.
