## Conclusion

The 21 November report's statement **100% Traceable / Corroborated** is not supported by the published source register. In this spot audit, only two of the nine listed source records were opened at their cited URLs and found to support the corresponding general claims. One source URL returned 404, the Microsoft SEC citation did not identify the actual filing, and five other company-specific citations could not be independently resolved or matched to the exact claimed source.

Six of the nine listed source publication dates precede the report's stated coverage window of 18–21 November. The two Ars Technica articles that did resolve were dated 20 November and 19 November, not 21 November as the register states.

This review does not conclude that every unsupported claim is false. It concludes that the claims are **not verified by the sources recorded in the report** and must not be treated as confirmed investment facts until individually re-sourced.

## Microsoft filing correction

The source register cited a Microsoft Form 10-Q as a 12 November filing and linked to an accession that does not match the actual September 2026 quarterly filing.

The actual SEC filing is:

- **Form:** 10-Q
- **Filing date:** 28 October 2026
- **Accession:** 0001193125-26-436133
- **Official filing:** [Microsoft Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526436133/msft-20260930.htm)

The filing's cash-flow statement reports **$39.367 billion** of additions to property and equipment for the three months ended 30 September 2026, compared with **$19.394 billion** in the corresponding 2025 quarter. It does not support the prior report's stated $19.2 billion quarterly figure, the asserted 68% cloud-infrastructure allocation, or the asserted 60-region expansion.

Separately, the filing reports **$364.6 billion** of additional leases, primarily for data centres, that had not yet commenced as of 30 September 2026. Some arrangements are subject to contractual conditions. This is a material commitment disclosure, but it must not be confused with cash capex already incurred.

## Implications for the archive

1. Do not use the 21 November report's unsupported NVIDIA, TSMC, AWS, SK hynix, Vertiv, Google or Microsoft figures as confirmed facts.
2. Re-source each material claim from the relevant company's official newsroom, investor-relations page, SEC filing or another opened primary source before reintroducing it.
3. Keep the 21 November publication unchanged. This review is a dated correction record, not a silent rewrite.
4. Do not repeat the 100% traceability label for that publication.
5. The 22 November interim addendum was separately checked during this scan. Its SEC NVIDIA earnings release and two Reuters articles were opened and support the claims attributed to them.

## Audit limitations

This is a source-register and high-impact-claim spot audit of the 21 November publication, not a complete re-audit of every historical Infotag report. An inaccessible URL is classified as unverified, not automatically false. The Microsoft filing mismatch, TSMC 404 response and the two Ars Technica publication-date discrepancies are directly observed.

**Disposition:** The 21 November report remains archived, but its unsupported company-specific figures are quarantined from use as verified investment evidence until re-sourced.
