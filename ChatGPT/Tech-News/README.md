# Tech News

The Tech News collection has two complementary layers:

1. **Chronological archive:** what happened during a specific reporting period.
2. **Permanent research library:** what is known about a company, industry, or investment thesis across time.

## Sections

- [01 — Frequency](01-Frequency/README.md): three-day reporting batches.
- [02 — Master Reports](02-Master-Reports/README.md): consolidated research.
- [03 — Company Research](03-Company-Research/README.md): company-specific dossiers.
- [04 — Industry Analysis](04-Industry-Analysis/README.md): sector research.
- [05 — Investment Outlook](05-Investment-Outlook/README.md): forward-looking scenarios.
- [06 — Sources and References](06-Sources-and-References/README.md): evidence register.
- [07 — Archive](07-Archive/README.md): superseded and historical reports.

Keep subject-based sections outside the frequency archive. A dated batch records the news as it was reported; the permanent library accumulates context and updated analysis.
