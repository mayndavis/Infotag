# Frequency Archive

## Every three days

Each reporting batch is a self-contained record of the period. Use this naming convention:

`YYYY-MM-DD_to_YYYY-MM-DD`

Store batches under `Every-3-Days/YYYY/Month/`. Each batch should cover:

- Major technology and market developments.
- Company-specific positive and negative developments.
- AI, semiconductors, cloud, data centres, networking, energy and cooling.
- Market reaction and relevant financial data.
- Investor implications and changes, if any, to the 2027–2031 outlook.
- Sources, publication dates, and confidence labels.

Do not silently edit an old batch when new information emerges. Add a correction note or a revised edition that links to the original.
